## 1. Pain Clusters

### By Frequency

**Daily Pains:**
1. **Contractor Management** (Score: 9/10)
   - Coordinating multiple contractors across multiple projects
   - Tracking contractor progress and quality
   - Managing contractor communication (phone, text, email)
   - Handling contractor delays and issues
   - **Frequency**: Daily (every active project, every contractor interaction)
   - **Impact**: Blocks project completion, causes delays, increases costs

2. **Budget Tracking** (Score: 8/10)
   - Tracking expenses across multiple projects
   - Monitoring budget vs. actual spending
   - Identifying budget overruns early
   - **Frequency**: Daily/Weekly (every expense, every project update)
   - **Impact**: Budget overruns reduce ROI, cause financial stress

**Weekly Pains:**
3. **Expense Organization** (Score: 6/10)
   - Categorizing expenses for tax purposes
   - Organizing receipts and documentation
   - Preparing expense reports
   - **Frequency**: Weekly (ongoing expense tracking)
   - **Impact**: Time-consuming, error-prone, critical for tax compliance

**Event-Driven Pains:**
4. **Deal Analysis Speed and Confidence** (Score: 7/10)
   - Quickly analyzing potential deals
   - Calculating accurate ARV (After Repair Value)
   - Determining MAO (Maximum Allowable Offer)
   - Validating deal assumptions
   - **Frequency**: Event-driven (during property acquisition phase)
   - **Impact**: Slow analysis means missed deals, inaccurate analysis means bad deals

5. **Portfolio-wide ROI Visibility** (Score: 5/10)
   - Tracking ROI across all active projects
   - Comparing performance across projects
   - Identifying best-performing strategies
   - **Frequency**: Monthly (portfolio review)
   - **Impact**: Limited strategic insights, missed optimization opportunities
